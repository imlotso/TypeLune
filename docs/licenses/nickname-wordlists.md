# Nickname wordlist attribution

The Chinese (`zh`) and English (`en`) wordlists are from [LDNOOBW / Wordnik](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words/tree/5faf2ba42d7b1c0977169ec3611df25a3c08eb13), source revision `5faf2ba42d7b1c0977169ec3611df25a3c08eb13`. They are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the full license is included in [wordnik-cc-by-4.0.txt](wordnik-cc-by-4.0.txt).

TypeLune separates them into editable language packs, removes duplicate lines and adds rule IDs, enabled states and matching modes. Chinese has 318 unique source entries and English has 403. The filtering feature and both selections are disabled initially; an administrator can inspect, add, edit, delete, import and export before enabling it. Source terms may depend on context; matching modes are configurable. Exports retain attribution. No remote wordlist is fetched during normal operation.
